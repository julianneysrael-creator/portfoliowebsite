End-to-end marketing for Pampers Aircon Pants, across media, retail, and e-commerce

CompanyP&G Pampers
My roleBrand Management Intern
TimeframeJanuary 2025 – April 2025

context.

I interned in Brand Management at Procter & Gamble Philippines, where I led an end-to-end growth strategy for Pampers Aircon Pants.

challenge.

Grow Pampers Aircon Pants by 35% with one plan that covers the whole funnel, from media and creators to the shelf and the TikTok Live screen, and that speaks to how today’s millennial moms shop.

what i did.

  • Consumer insight: interviewed 20 mothers nationwide and ran surveys to find the core insight: moms work hard to keep toddlers cool, but don’t realize their diaper still traps heat.
  • Data: analyzed shipment, market share, and distribution data across customers and merchants to size the opportunity and back every recommendation.
  • Consumer cohorts: built 4 distinct cohorts (Lifestyle, Weather-Wary, Trend-Chasing, and Quality-First Moms), each with its own creators, cooling behaviors, trial barrier, and message.
  • Media: wrote the KOL and influencer plan (a 5-pager), a content plan and content mandatories for each cohort, ideas for events and activations, and creative media executions.
  • Retail: recommended distribution, promotion, shelving, and merchandising plans, including tracking distribution per customer.
  • E-commerce: planned the Aircon Pants activation: livestream maximization, messaging, visuals, and an affiliate incubation plan with competitive commission.
  • Design: created updated TikTok Live graphics and condensed the e-commerce pages from 9 to 5.
  • Campaign concepts: came up with ways to scale current campaigns and make them more relevant to millennial moms.

results.

35%growth target
20mothers interviewed nationwide
4consumer cohorts with tailored content plans
9 → 5e-commerce pages, condensed
