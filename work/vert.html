Context

Vert App Studios is a mobile app studio. I ran its creator partnership program end to end and owned the brand’s Instagram.

Challenge

Growing an app portfolio through creators means many partnerships running at once. That needs sourcing, negotiation, briefs, approvals, and reporting that don’t live in someone’s inbox.

What I did

  • Ran 80+ creator partnerships through the full lifecycle: sourcing, outreach, negotiation, briefing, approvals, and reporting.
  • Built the Vert Content Hub, a system that organizes 300+ creators so the program can scale without adding headcount.
  • Ran the brand Instagram: content concepts, captions, approvals, and tone of voice.
